Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GRYD handles personal information when you use our website and services. GRYD is operated from Spain and is intended for people who can access the service worldwide, with a focus on Spain and the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA).

1. Who is responsible

GRYD is operated from Spain. For questions or requests about personal information, contact us at privacy@yourdomain.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information, including your email address and account identifier.
  • Your game library, including game identifiers, titles, statuses, and when entries were added.
  • An optional profile picture you choose to upload.
  • Technical request information processed by our infrastructure providers, such as IP address, request time, and browser or device details.

Supabase Auth processes credentials to authenticate your account. GRYD does not store passwords in its game-library database.

3. Why we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • Save and display your game library and profile picture.
  • Operate, maintain, and protect GRYD.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to support requests.

For people in the EEA, the relevant legal basis is generally providing the service you request, our legitimate interest in operating and securing it, or compliance with a legal obligation, as applicable.

4. Service providers and game data

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, and profile picture storage. The project database is configured in Paris, France.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website and backend. Its network may process technical request information in different locations.
  • GRYD uses IGDB to retrieve video-game catalogue information. Catalogue requests are made by the backend; GRYD does not send your account credentials to IGDB.

Providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. Depending on how a provider operates its service, technical information may be processed outside your country or the EEA. Where required, applicable safeguards for international transfers will be used.

5. Cookies and local storage

GRYD uses a strictly necessary, secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your display theme preference in your browser's local storage; this preference is not an authentication cookie.

Because GRYD does not currently use optional analytics or advertising cookies, there is no separate Cookie Policy. Infrastructure providers may process technical data as described above.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep account information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. You can permanently delete your account from the account settings in GRYD. This also deletes its saved games and uploaded profile pictures. Provider security logs or backups may be retained for limited periods under the provider's policies or where required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to restrict or object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it. To make a request, contact privacy@yourdomain.com.

If you are in Spain, you may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), without prejudice to other rights available to you.

8. Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy and will publish the revised version and date on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@yourdomain.com.
